# 04 - Create and Publish a News Post

## Goal

Create and publish a news post on my site. I used it to post a quick guide for people on how to upload files and folders to SharePoint, based on what I did in [**03 - Create and upload Files and Folders**](03-create-and-upload-files-and-folder.md).

## Environment

- Microsoft 365 through my school account
- Site: SharePoint Practice Lab (team site from [**01 - Create a Team Site**](01-create-team-site.md))

---

### 1. Sign in to Microsoft 365

Go to [https://m365.cloud.microsoft](https://m365.cloud.microsoft) and sign in.

<img width="933" height="659" alt="1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd64d7d4-acee-4657-986f-0c6afe1b9a57" />

### 2. Open SharePoint

Click the app launcher (the 9 dots, top left) and select **SharePoint**.

<img width="917" height="557" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4705d4a8-994b-4468-894f-4ec067e0f3ce" />

<img width="1096" height="682" alt="3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eaee35ee-70a2-4390-bac4-c22869ae16a8" />

### 1. Go to Publish

Click **Create → News post**. (The other option, **Page**, is for regular pages that don't show up as news.)

<img width="910" height="477" alt="4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/25516d2a-9c3a-44ef-a6ee-c492d23ed278" />

### 3. Pick a template

The **Template gallery** has templates like Visual topic introduction, Topic overview, Monthly news update, Article and Resources. I searched for and picked **Organization-wide update**, which is made for sharing announcements and updates. 

<img width="1021" height="697" alt="5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dd8c511d-a01b-433e-ba57-14793af034b7" />

<img width="1244" height="545" alt="6" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6ac82212-63cc-411f-9883-c572a0f4e6ce" />

Click **Use this template**.

<img width="973" height="614" alt="7" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/49a76294-8055-44bd-b733-a9115315effe" />

### 4. Choose the site

In **Choose where to create**, select the site (SharePoint Practice Lab) and click **Create**. This opens the news post in edit mode.

<img width="528" height="302" alt="8" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5169fc2-9a13-4b86-8d71-93848ebe3178" />

<img width="473" height="282" alt="9" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb7132ff-07ec-4e40-b9fd-6ff82d28ee07" />

### 5. Edit the heading

The template comes with sample content ("Highlights from last fiscal year"). Click the heading and type your own. I changed it to **How to upload a file/folder to SharePoint**.

Click the first section and open **Properties**. Under **Columns**, I changed it from **Two columns** to **One column**.
