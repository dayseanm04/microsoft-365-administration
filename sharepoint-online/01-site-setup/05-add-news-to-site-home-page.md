# 05 - Add News to the Site Home Page

## Goal

Show my news post on the site home page so it's the first thing people see when they open the site. In [**02 - Customize the Site Home Page**](02-customize-site-home-page.md) I removed the News web part. Here I add it back.

## Environment

- Microsoft 365 through my school account
- Site: SharePoint Practice Lab (team site from [**01 - Create a Team Site**](01-create-team-site.md))
- News post from [**04 - Create a News Post**](04-create-news-post.md)

---

## Steps

### 1. Sign in to Microsoft 365

Go to [https://m365.cloud.microsoft](https://m365.cloud.microsoft) and sign in.

<img width="933" height="659" alt="1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd64d7d4-acee-4657-986f-0c6afe1b9a57" />

### 2. Open SharePoint

Click the app launcher (the 9 dots, top left) and select **SharePoint**.

<img width="917" height="557" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4705d4a8-994b-4468-894f-4ec067e0f3ce" />

<img width="1096" height="682" alt="3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eaee35ee-70a2-4390-bac4-c22869ae16a8" />

### 3. Open the site and go into edit mode

Open the site from **Recent sites** on the SharePoint start page, then click **Edit** (top right).

<img width="1370" height="620" alt="4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c24b019e-46ff-444e-af4d-a612f2ed427e" />

<img width="454" height="151" alt="5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/094070a7-34dd-4e82-9471-7b3da455b464" />

### 4. Remove the Quick links web part

Click the **Quick links** section and click the **trash can** to delete it.

<img width="949" height="447" alt="6" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/54c1b642-af6e-4e35-8b69-3fde11ec765a" />

### 5. Add a new section

Hover at the top of the page and click **+ Section**. Section options include Flexible, One column, Two columns, Three columns, One-third left, One-third right, Full-width and Vertical. I picked **Flexible**.

<img width="938" height="313" alt="7" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6d044619-94a1-41e5-a620-9741922725ed" />

<img width="322" height="219" alt="8" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/566d42e2-faa8-4898-91a1-67047053cd51" />

### 6. Add the News web part

Click the **Toolbox** icon on the right and select **News** under Web parts. My news post shows up right away.

<img width="681" height="398" alt="9" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/51d83ca7-317c-480d-9b73-ee5056d89d1f" />

<img width="708" height="618" alt="10" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73a54534-1c86-4032-b0a7-662383cf012f" />

### 7. Pick a layout

Click **Layout options** in the small toolbar. Options are Top story, List, Side-by-side, Hub News, Carousel, Tiles and Filmstrip. I picked **Top story** so the post is big and easy to see.

<img width="697" height="724" alt="11" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aa5a417d-ba0f-4c7e-b8f0-e9a37c313079" />

### 6. Hide the "+ Add" button

The News section shows a **+ Add** button by default. Open **Edit properties** and, under **Layout**, turn **Show commands** off. This hides the button so visitors just see the news.
