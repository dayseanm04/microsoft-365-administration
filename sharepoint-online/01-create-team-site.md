# 01 - Create a SharePoint Team Site

## Goal
Create a private team site to use as my practice lab for learning SharePoint administration: permissions, sharing, libraries, lists and recovery.

## Environment
- Microsoft 365 through my school account
- Since it's a school tenant and not an admin account, I don't have access to every feature (no SharePoint admin center)

---

## Steps

### 1. Sign in to Microsoft 365

Go to [https://m365.cloud.microsoft](https://m365.cloud.microsoft) and sign in.

### 2. Open SharePoint

Click the app launcher (the 9 dots, top left) and select **SharePoint**.
